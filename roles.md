## Vacancies

The Constitution requires more offices than are currently filled:

- **Deputy Agent** — §3.2 provides for the Agent and the Deputy Agent to be elected separately. No Deputy Agent has been appointed, so §3.3 (the Deputy assuming the Agent's duties) and §3.9 (the Deputy acting on a recall) cannot operate.
- **Deputies for Convener and Treasurer** — the Constitution provides that the Convener, Treasurer and Record Keepers "shall be held by two members, a primary and a deputy". Only the Record Keeper role currently has two holders.

Where a required role is unfilled, the Convener temporarily performs it until it is filled through the applicable process (Constitution, *Interim responsibility for unfilled roles*).

## No selection process exists for three of these roles

§3.2 provides a method for the Agent and the Deputy Agent only. For the Convener, Treasurer and Record Keepers the Constitution states that they are mandatory rotating roles with two-month terms, that rotation *schedules* are decided by Policy resolution, and that selection should "strike a balance between members' preferences, skill development opportunities, and existing experience" — but it nowhere states **how** they are selected.

§6 supplied a process for the interim appointment. That process was specific to September 2026 and expires with the interim period. **Until §3.2 is amended or a Policy adopted, the Laboratory has no method by which to elect a Convener, Treasurer or Record Keeper.**
