What you are filing decides where it goes, and where it goes decides which procedure applies. Pick the one that matches what this actually does — not the quickest route. Each opens a new file in GitHub, already filled in.

Amending the Constitution is Layer 4 and edits constitution.md itself rather than adding a file. When you commit, choose “create a new branch and start a pull request”.

Read live from the Constitutional Record on each load, and from the Constitution itself, which is checked against the figures above. Times are UK time, as the Constitution states them; a countdown runs from the opening recorded on the proposal, not from when it was filed.

What this page cannot see is the deliberation — who said what in the governance channel, and who objected. It reports counts and thresholds and stops: whether a proposal carried is a Record Keeper's finding, not a web page's.
