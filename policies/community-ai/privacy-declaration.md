# Emys — privacy declaration

*Published with the Community AI policy. It states, in plain terms, what Emys reads, keeps, and discards, and the choices you have. It is not operative; where it and the policy differ, the policy governs.*

## What Emys reads

- The **shared channels** of the Laboratory's Discord server, by default.
- The Laboratory's **instruments** — library, room booking, calendar, coordination spreadsheets, topic.forum, and the Constitutional Record — **read-only**.
- Your **private workspace** and your **direct messages** with Emys — see *What stays private*.

## What Emys keeps in the shared community memory

- Only **factual community knowledge** from shared channels — events, schedules, logistics, resources, decisions — each with its **source named**.
- It does **not** keep opinions, arguments, complaints about people, third-party claims about anyone, or anyone's health, relationships, or personal circumstances.

## What stays private

- Your workspace, your direct messages with Emys, and your personal memory are **private to you**, and are **not** placed in the shared community memory during this experiment.
- You can have your personal memory **corrected or deleted** on request.

## Your choices

- Ask an administrator to **exclude any channel** from Emys, and it will be excluded.
- A **personal-feed channel** is read only if you **opt in**.

## Where your data is processed

- Emys is run by two administrators, @emergentvibe and @anchitsom. Neither acts alone, and neither reads your private workspace, messages, or memory **except to investigate a specific bug or incident**.
- Emys runs on the Laboratory's **own compute**, in the house, with **open-weights models** and **no external logging** of your content — from the day it is deployed. No member content is sent to an external model service.
- Processing is the **minimum** each function needs, and nothing more.
- Emys records **how often and how much it is used** — counts of queries and of who uses it, not the content of what you ask — to measure the experiment. Any deeper look at *how you personally* use Emys, to improve it, happens **only if you opt in** when an administrator asks, and you can withdraw at any time.

## What this does not change

- The College keeps its legal obligations, including **data protection** (Charter §8). This declaration operates within them and changes none.

## When it ends

- The experiment ends **29 January 2027** (or with the Constitution, if that is sooner). Emys then stops reading and answering; the shared community memory is kept **frozen** unless the Laboratory directs otherwise; and your private workspace and memory are deleted on request.
