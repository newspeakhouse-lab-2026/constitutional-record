# Emys — privacy declaration

*A summary of the Community AI policy for members. Not operative: where this and the policy differ, the policy governs. The numbers in brackets are the policy's clauses.*

**Who runs it.** Not the Laboratory. One member, **@emergentvibe**, runs Emys on a server he rents, using software he wrote and owns (3.1, 4.1, 11.1). The Laboratory pays the costs and sets the terms he must run it on; it holds no access to the machine and no rights in the code (3.1, 4.2).

**What that means for your data.** He can technically reach everything stored on that machine, and no other member can check what he does there. He undertakes not to read your workspace, your messages with Emys, or your personal memory, except to investigate a bug or incident someone has reported (10.1(b)). That is a promise with a remedy behind it, not a technical barrier — the policy says so in as many words. If your content is ever accessed without authorisation, or lost, he must tell the Laboratory without delay (10.1(j)).

**What it reads and keeps.** The shared Discord channels, and the Laboratory's instruments read-only (1.2, 7.1). From the channels it stores only attributed facts — events, schedules, logistics, resources, decisions — never opinions, arguments, complaints about people, or anyone's health, relationships or circumstances (6.1, 6.2). Your workspace and your messages with Emys stay private and never enter the shared memory (5.1, 5.2).

**What it was started with.** Public documents — the Record, the cohort website and newsletter, the fellowship charter — the existing shared-channel messages under the same limits, and your own published profile in the programme directory, removable on request (5.3, 6.4). Personal-feed channels are excluded unless you opted in. Emys also holds a map of who is who — Discord IDs, display names, personal channel IDs, GitHub handles — in a private repository visible to the cohort.

**Where it goes.** Stored and processed in the United Kingdom. The open-weights models that answer you run on the **East London GPU Coop's** GPUs, which keeps no logs — the coop's word, which the Laboratory cannot verify from outside. Nothing goes to a commercial AI provider; nothing is used to train or evaluate a model, sold, published, or used for any other community or client (8.1, 10.1(c), 10.1(d)).

**What you can do.**

- Have any channel excluded, and whatever was already taken from it removed (6.3).
- Keep a personal-feed channel out unless you opt in (6.3).
- Have your personal memory corrected or deleted (8.2).
- **Withdraw all your own content at any time, without a vote and without giving a reason (10.4).**
- Decline any analysis of how you personally use Emys — it needs your opt-in, and you can withdraw it (8.4).

**When it ends.** 29 January 2027, or with the Constitution if that is sooner. The shared memory is copied monthly to a store the Laboratory controls, readable without his software, so it survives both the experiment and him (10.6). On expiry he deletes members' content and confirms it in the governance channel (10.7).
