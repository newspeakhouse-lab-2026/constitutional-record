# Rule: Dispatches cadence and rotation

**Layer:** 2
**Status:** live

*Filed together with `policy.md` as part of the Layer 3 proposal that establishes the Dispatches policy area — its initial adoption runs on that proposal's Layer 3 threshold, not a standalone Layer 2 tier. A future amendment to this file alone, leaving `policy.md` untouched, is a Layer 2 Ordinary change.*

This sets the tunable cadence, rotation, and evaluation parameters for the policy in `policy.md`. Retuning it does not require reopening that policy.

## 1. Submission window

The shared drafting document opens Thursday morning and stays open through Sunday night. The Editor assembles the issue Monday and Tuesday.

## 2. Cadence

*Dispatches* launches **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**, and publishes every Wednesday morning after that, timed to land before that evening's Ration Club.

*The original draft proposed launching 14 October 2026; that date has passed without the policy being adopted. This recomputes the whole schedule forward from today, 6 November 2026, rather than copying the stale dates.*

The publishing platform (Ghost, per `rule-ghost-publishing.md`) needs to support sending the weekly issue to a subscriber list, donations or paid subscriptions, and an archive for material that does not fit inside a Wednesday issue — full interviews, write-ups, and other Laboratory publications.

## 3. Roles and rotation

Two rotating roles, each held by every Fellow exactly once over the Charter, in three-week terms:

- **Editor** — sets the week's structure and assembles the issue.
- **Publisher** — the round immediately before their own Editor term; reviews each issue alongside the Agent, publishes it, and handles the rest of the site.

Claiming a round as Editor also claims the previous round's Publisher slot — one sign-up fills both, so there is never a separate scheduling clash. Round 1 is the exception: it starts cold, with no round before it to shadow, and in exchange that Editor also becomes Round 14's Publisher, so every claimed round still ends with both roles filled exactly once.

Once this policy is adopted, the first Editor facilitates a sign-up for Fellows to claim a round. First come, first served. Any two Fellows may swap after claiming, by mutual agreement.

**If a round's Editor slot is unclaimed a week before it starts**, anyone in the Laboratory may claim it on short notice, or that round's already-claimed Publisher may run it solo rather than leave an issue unpublished.

**If an Editor or Publisher goes silent mid-term with no notice**, the other role-holder may publish alone rather than let an issue lapse. A repeated pattern is a matter for the ordinary conflict process, not this rule.

## 4. Evaluation

The current Publisher reviews open-rate, subscriber-growth, and tip-jar numbers monthly — not chased weekly, since a single low-open week or a quiet donation month says little on its own.

If a round's Editor and Publisher both go quiet with no notice, the following round's pair may start early rather than let issues lapse. How the missed weeks are made up, if at all, is for the Laboratory to sort out when it actually happens, not something to over-specify here.
