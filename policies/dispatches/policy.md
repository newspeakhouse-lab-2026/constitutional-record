# Policy: Dispatches

**Layer:** 3
**Status:** live

The Laboratory adopts this policy under the Laboratory's Constitution. The Constitution takes priority over this policy.

## 1. What this governs

This policy governs *Dispatches*: the Laboratory's weekly newsletter to Faculty, the Fellowship, and other acquaintances of Newspeak House. It sets out how material is gathered, how an issue is edited and reviewed, and how that work rotates across the cohort so it does not collapse onto the same one or two people every week.

It does not cover:

- **Which platform the newsletter runs on.** That was decided separately — `rules/rule-ghost-publishing.md` adopts Ghost for both the cohort site and the newsletter, and is already live.
- **The Laboratory's other public channels**, except where they intersect with weekly or site publication.
- **The Laboratory's overall editorial line on external topics** — only the process by which its own story gets told.
- **Non-newsletter content on the site** beyond one suggestion recorded here: a page of Fellowship Candidate biographies (see §4.6).
- **The disclaimer every edition carries** — already adopted separately as `rules/rule-dispatches-disclaimer.md`.

## 2. Source of authority

The Charter's Findings provisions require the Laboratory to publish its findings — experiments, visitors, fieldnotes, and member activities. *The exact Charter section number and wording have not been confirmed against the primary document and should be checked before this is filed.*

This policy claims no authority over anything Charter §8 reserves to the College. In particular: the financial arrangement at §3.5 is managed jointly with the Treasurer under the Laboratory's own financial policy, not independently by the Editor or Publisher, so it creates no new Laboratory financial authority beyond what the Treasury policy already governs.

## 3. Who decides, and what constrains them

This policy creates two rotating roles. Nobody holds both at once, and every Fellow holds each exactly once over the course of the Charter.

- **Editor** — sets the week's structure, runs the submission window, and assembles and writes the issue.
- **Publisher** — reviews each issue alongside the Agent before it goes out, does the actual publishing, and handles the rest of the site (other pages, the subscriber list, promotion of Laboratory events).

Filling, term length, the rotation order, and what happens when a slot goes unclaimed are tunable parameters, not durable frame — they are set out in the companion `rule-cadence-rotation.md` in this folder, and may be retuned there at Layer 2 without reopening this policy.

The Record Keepers add the Editor and Publisher to `roles.md` once this policy is adopted, and update the current holders at the start of each round.

Disputes about whether an item should run at all — as opposed to how it is framed — go to the ordinary proposal process, not to the Editor, Publisher, or Agent individually.

## 4. The rules themselves

### 4.1 Gathering material

During the submission window (set in `rule-cadence-rotation.md`), anyone may add material two ways: writing directly into the shared drafting document, or posting in `#dispatches` tagged `ADDITION`. Either way, it is there for the Editor to draw from.

`#dispatches` is the raw feed, not the newsletter. Nothing in it is published unedited — the Editor treats it as material to gather and synthesise, not a live chat log to republish.

### 4.2 Editorial process

At the start of their term, the Editor posts a short prompt to `#dispatches` setting that week's shape — what sections the issue needs, and which items from the intake feed look worth featuring. The format need not be the same every week; it is meant to be experimental.

The drafting document is open through the submission window. Anyone may write directly into the structure the Editor laid out, not only flag things for someone else to write up.

The Editor assembles, cuts, and writes the transitions once the window closes. Voice stays first person plural and concrete over promotional — as willing to report a stalled experiment or an open conflict as a clean win, since the Laboratory's governance arrangements are exactly the findings the Charter commits it to publishing.

AI tools may help the Editor pull together a first pass from the week's intake, but the editorial judgement — what runs, what it says, the voice — stays with the Editor. This role is not meant to be replaced by an aggregation script. Any AI use follows the Laboratory's AI use policy, if one exists, and common sense about privacy in the meantime.

### 4.3 Review

Before an edition goes out, the Publisher and the Agent both read the draft: the Publisher as a second pair of eyes on the copy, the Agent because *Dispatches* goes out under the Laboratory's name.

This read is also the last checkpoint for objections. If a member flags that something written about them should not run as written, it is held until resolved — never published over an objection.

### 4.4 Cadence and subscription

Publication day, the launch date, and what the publishing platform needs to support (subscriber lists, an archive, donations or paid subscriptions) are set out in `rule-cadence-rotation.md`. Any member of the public may subscribe, not only Faculty and the Fellowship, though they remain the primary audience.

### 4.5 Record-keeping

The published archive, not the intake channel, is the record that counts if a dispatch is ever cited in a dispute or audit. Per the Rule of Recognition, a record needs to be durable, inspectable, and tamper-evident, which a chat log is not. Where a dispatch touches a decision or judgement rather than news, the Laboratory's constitutional record remains the authoritative copy, not the newsletter.

### 4.6 Open question

Should the site include a page of Fellowship Candidate biographies? This policy does not decide it — it is recorded here as a suggestion raised during drafting, for the Laboratory to resolve separately.

## 5. Review

The rotation schedule, term length, and cadence are proposed defaults, expected to change once the Laboratory has run a few months on real data — see `rule-cadence-rotation.md` for how they are retuned. This policy itself is reviewed no later than the Interim Constitution's own re-ratification or expiry.

**Observed by:**

- Publication, or a missed issue, is visible in the public newsletter archive each week.
- Role assignments are visible in the rotation table in `rule-cadence-rotation.md` and in `roles.md`.
- The review read and any held items are noted in `#dispatches`.
- Income is visible in the Treasurer's financial record.
