# Policy: Guest Room

**Layer:** 3 · **Status:** proposed

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 The guest room is a relationship machine, not a bed. Newspeak House runs on intimacy, not transaction — shared meals, shared work, people who come to know each other. The guest room is where that gets built: a visiting speaker becomes someone you cooked with, a curious stranger becomes an applicant, an alumnus becomes a friend. This policy governs it (Room 5), whose stewardship the Charter devolves to the Laboratory (§3): who may stay, how a stay is approved, what guests are asked to contribute, and who is accountable for a guest being received.

1.2 It supersedes the interim arrangement in the Booking Policy (§8), under which the guest room could be used only by a Fellowship Candidate through 48-hour lazy consensus.

1.3 Stays are booked through the house booking system and cost no booking credits.

## 2. Who may stay, and the sponsor

2.1 In this policy a **member** is a Fellowship Candidate of the Laboratory; Faculty are not members, and the members together are the **cohort**. A **sponsor** backs a stay and is accountable for it, and may be a member or a Faculty member.

2.2 Members and Faculty book directly and are the sponsor of what they book. Someone who cannot log in uses the request form, which must name a sponsor; a request with no sponsor is not processed.

2.3 Being named as a sponsor does not by itself bind that person. A named sponsor is told of the naming, and their backing takes effect only if they do not **disown** the stay within the objection window (§3.1); a disown ends the stay. No one is made accountable for a stay they did not agree to back. Disowning one's own stay is open to any sponsor, member or Faculty, and is distinct from the general objection of §3.1 — which only members may raise.

2.4 The sponsor is accountable for the stay: for the guest's conduct in the house, and for their reception (Section 5).

2.5 Each request carries a short **introduction** from the guest — who they are and what they would like to share with the house. It is invited, not required, and it opens the welcome in Section 5.

## 3. How a stay is approved

3.1 Every guest-room request — a member's booking, or a sponsored request from the form — is posted to the cohort through the booking system and confirmed by **silent consent**: it stands unless a member objects within the objection window (set in the booking system, currently 48 hours). **Only members may object** to a stay; a named sponsor may separately **disown** their own stay (§2.3), whether or not they are a member. **One stated objection — or a disown by the named sponsor — ends the request.** The requester is told the outcome either way.

3.2 A request must be made with at least the minimum notice the booking system requires (currently three days), except for the short-notice stays in §3.3.

3.3 **Short-notice stays.** When a night is otherwise unbooked, a sponsor may confirm a stay at short notice — down to the same day — for **up to 2 nights**, without waiting out the objection window, so a room that would sit empty can be filled; the sponsor takes personal responsibility for receiving the guest (Section 5). A stay of **more than 2 nights is not confirmed this way** — it goes through the normal approval of §3.1 in full, where a member may object. So a short-notice guest may stay up to two nights on the sponsor's word; a longer stay needs the cohort's silent consent.

3.4 **Maximum stay.** A single stay is at most **two weeks (fourteen nights)**. A stay beyond that is not booked through this policy and would need the cohort's agreement separately.

## 4. Contributions

4.1 Guests are kindly asked to contribute to the Laboratory for their stay, on a sliding scale by what they can afford.

| Rate | Per night | Choose this if you are… |
|---|---|---|
| **Supported** | £30 | on a low or uncertain income — a student, early-career, or having a tight month |
| **Standard** | £60 | able to cover the cost of your stay comfortably |
| **Solidarity** | £120 | doing well, and glad to keep the room open and affordable for others |

4.2 Contributions are received through the Laboratory's Open Collective, per the Financial Policy.

4.3 A member staying in the room on their own account is not asked to contribute but may do so if they wish.

## 5. The greeting, access, and the room

5.1 A guest is not entitled to unsupervised entry to the house and is not added to the College's Access Register (Charter §7): a guest is **received in person**.

5.2 **The sponsor is accountable for the guest being received** — met on arrival, let in, and shown the room. The sponsor need not do it themselves and may arrange for another member to; but if no one receives the guest, that is the sponsor's failing.

5.3 Laundering the bed linen and the turnaround between guests are the sponsor's own responsibility. The sponsor may arrange another member to do it.

5.4 When a stay is confirmed, the cohort is told a guest is coming.

5.5 A guest is given a second point of contact, in case neither the sponsor nor a delegate appears to receive them.

## 6. Implementation

6.1 Bringing the booking system into line with this policy is the responsibility of **@anchitsom**, who built and runs it, within **three weeks of this policy's adoption**. The changes required are listed in `implementation.md`.

**Observed by:** the booking system's calendar and each posted request are visible to the cohort; contributions are visible on the Open Collective; whether a guest is received is seen in the ordinary course, and is the sponsor's account to give; and whether the system was brought into line by the Section 6 deadline is visible in the system itself.
