# Rule: Co-Safety Leads for the current term

**Layer:** 2
**Tier:** A
**Status:** live

**Ends:** 23:59 UK time, Monday 30 November 2026 (`2026-11-30T23:59:00+00:00`) — the two-month term §8 of the safeguarding and wellbeing Policy sets, or the expiry of this Constitution under §5, whichever comes first

## 1. Appointment

Tess Buckley and Fernanda Munhoz are the two Co-Safety Leads under §8 of the
safeguarding and wellbeing Policy.

They hold the office co-equally. Neither is a deputy to the other, and either may
act alone where that section gives the Co-Safety Leads a function.

## 2. Term

§8 sets a two-month term. No office outlasts the Constitution that creates it, so
this appointment ends no later than the instant in the `Ends:` line above. If the
Constitution is re-ratified or replaced before then, the two-month term runs from
adoption of this rule, and a further rule is needed to extend or refill the office
beyond it.

## 3. Entry in the Record

On adoption, the Record Keepers enter both names against the two Co-Safety Lead
rows in `roles.md`, with the term end this rule states.

## 4. Vacancy

If either holder steps down, or is removed under §8, the office is vacant and
`roles.md` records it as vacant. Refilling it is a further rule under §8's
ordinary rule process. The remaining holder continues to act alone until then.

**Observed by:** the Co-Safety Leads are the published first point of contact, so
whether the office is filled is apparent from `roles.md` and from the Safety
Concern Form's routing. Whether the holders act is apparent to the person who
raised a concern, who §5.1 of the Policy entitles to an acknowledgement within 48
hours. This authorises no new surveillance and no record beyond the restricted
file §9 of the Policy already provides for.
