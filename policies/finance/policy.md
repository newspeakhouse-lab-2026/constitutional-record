# Laboratory Financial Policy

**Layer:** 3
**Status:** live

The Laboratory adopts this policy under the Laboratory's Constitution. The Constitution takes priority over this policy.

## Source of authority

Constitution §1 (Layers): "Budgets, custody arrangements, reimbursement procedures and routine expense handling may be governed by Policy or Ordinary Rules where consistent with those constitutional controls."

Constitution §3 (Other roles): "The Laboratory shall adopt Ordinary Rules governing custody of funds, withdrawals and payments, delegation of money for authorised spending, spending limits, receipts, return of unused money, reimbursement and reconciliation of accounts."

This policy was drafted by the Interim Treasurer.

## 1. Fiscal host and financial management

We propose using The Social Change Nest (SCN), a fiscal host, and its Open Collective platform to hold the Laboratory's funds in an inclusive, secure and responsible way. Although setting up an account with SCN involves considerable cost and effort, we see it as a way to support and engage with grassroots and alternative financial infrastructure that serves social impact and community building in London. Read more about SCN's fiscal hosting.

**Fees.** SCN charges a one-off £42 joining fee and 5% of all non-grant money we deposit. Both are paid from Laboratory funds.

**Admins.** The Treasurer, Deputy Treasurer and two signatories will be appointed as the Open Collective admins. Anyone leaving one of these roles loses access to the platform within 7 days.

**Income.** All money is received through Open Collective. The Laboratory's digital payment channels are linked directly to Open Collective, and any cash is passed to the Treasurer, who deposits it within 7 days.

**Approving payments.** Every expense needs approval from the Treasurer and one other signatory, who confirms by commenting on the expense in Open Collective. If the Treasurer is being paid or has a conflict, the Deputy Treasurer takes their place. Nobody approves an expense they submitted or will be paid from. Each expense names the budget or decision that approved it, and purchases must not be split up to get under a limit.

**Claiming money back.** Any member who has made a purchase on behalf of the Laboratory can claim the money back on Open Collective within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. Approved claims are paid within 14 days. Without a receipt, a written statement is accepted for up to £20.

**Leaving SCN.** Leaving SCN requires a majority vote of all members of the Laboratory.

## 2. Monthly expenses and budget

The Laboratory received £1,000 from Newspeak House at the Convention on 20 September 2026. This initial funding is currently being used to cover all the Laboratory's recurring and essential costs.

Based on conversations with previous fellowship cohorts and a review of the Laboratory's key activities, these are the recurring and essential costs the Laboratory will need to cover each month:

| Budget | Amount | Per month | Holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ration Club | £70 a week | £303 | Weekly Ration Club Lead |
| Website hosting | £11 a month | £11 | Infrastructure Lead |
| General (stationery and small costs) | £30 a month | £30 | Treasurer |
| **Total** | | **£344** | |

The Laboratory needs to raise about £362 a month to cover its basic recurring costs. This leaves £344 after SCN's 5% fee. Any other additions to the monthly budget will be considered only when the Laboratory's Open Collective account holds at least one and a half months of costs.

When the Laboratory has this surplus, any member can propose new spending by posting a short business case on Discord. The business case should cover:

- What they want to do and why.
- The total cost, itemised, and whether it is one-off or recurring.
- Who will do it and when.
- How we will know it worked.
- Any conflict of interest.

The proposal is then decided under Section 2 of the Constitution, through the procedure its layer requires. Where that is an Ordinary rule, it enters at Tier C: a simple majority of those who vote, with a quorum of half of all members. Within two weeks of finishing, the proposer reports back on Discord and returns any unspent money.

### Ration Club

Ration Club is a weekly communal meal at Newspeak House, managed by members of the Laboratory. The lead rotates every week and is responsible for ensuring the visibility of the digital and physical payment channels to accept donations from attendees. This financial policy provides a budget of £70 to the weekly Ration Club Lead for preparing the meal. Any additional expenses must be paid personally by that week's lead and the supporting members.

This policy also encourages members to use Ration Club to try different ways of encouraging attendees to donate. Ideas include casual, low-cost ticketed games such as Tambola, Lucky Draw and Quiz Night.

Donations made to Ration Club through digital payment channels go directly to Open Collective. Any cash donations are passed to the Treasurer, who deposits them within 7 days. Ration Club's weekly and monthly spending and donations will be included in the Treasurer's monthly financial statement.

### Website hosting

The Laboratory's tech infrastructure currently costs £11 a month and covers all Laboratory sites, including the newsletter. Any change or addition to this setup that affects the monthly budget will only be considered, and decided under Section 2 of the Constitution in the same way as new spending, once the Laboratory's Open Collective account holds at least one and a half months of recurring costs.

### General

Members can use this discretionary budget of £30 a month to buy food, stationery and everyday supplies. It must be spent only on things that make the shared space more comfortable for fellowship candidates' everyday use, such as coffee, tea, pens and paper. This policy approves the General budget. Each purchase within it goes ahead if no member objects within 48 hours of it being posted on Discord.

## 3. Raising money

The Laboratory raises funds on a not-for-profit basis. Any money raised is used to meet its recurring costs and to support exploratory political technology work undertaken by its members.

Within the remit set by the College, the Laboratory can raise money through Ration Club donations, newsletter subscriptions, bookings of the guest room and other communal spaces, and ticketed events at Newspeak House.

Laboratory members regard Newspeak House as shared communal infrastructure. Regular spaces at the venue can be booked free of charge, on a first-come, first-served basis, under the House's current Booking Policy. However, any member can propose an Ordinary rule (Constitution §2, Ordinary rules) to set a fee for people outside the Laboratory using the communal spaces. The proposer must notify residents directly while the proposal is open, so they can raise any potential conflict before its deliberation period closes.

The guest room, however, is charged and maintained as per the Guest Room Policy.

Beyond Ration Club, fellowship candidates are encouraged to run events at the venue and to invite attendees to support the Laboratory through direct donations, or indirectly through ticket sales or newsletter sign-ups.

## 4. Roles and records

| Role | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| Treasurer | Keeping the record, approving payments and reporting monthly |
| Deputy Treasurer | Standing in for the Treasurer when they are unavailable or have a conflict |
| Two signatories | Giving the second approval on payments |
| Weekly Ration Club Lead | Running that week's Ration Club within its £70 budget |
| Infrastructure Lead | Managing the website hosting budget |

The Treasurer and Deputy Treasurer are constitutional offices, filled under Constitution §3.2 by anonymous ranked-choice ballot for a two-month term. The signatories and Infrastructure Lead are appointed by a majority of all members. On the first working day of each month, the Treasurer posts the monthly financial statement on Discord, showing the balance, income, spending, what is left in each budget, and Ration Club's spending and donations via a Google document.

## 5. Review

This policy should be reviewed whenever the Constitution is re-ratified or replaced.

**Observed by:** expenses, approval comments and deposits are visible to members on Open Collective; the Treasurer's monthly statement is posted on Discord.
