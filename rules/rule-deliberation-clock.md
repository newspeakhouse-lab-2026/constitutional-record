# Rule: When a deliberation period begins

**Layer:** 2 — Ordinary
**Tier:** A — 48 hours
**Status:** live

## Why this is needed

Section 2 sets six deliberation procedures and says what starts only two of them. Tier A runs on the proposal being visible in the governance channel; a Constitutional amendment runs from two endorsing reviews. **Tier B, Tier C, Policy resolutions and Emergency resolutions name no trigger at all.**

A period nobody can date is a period nobody can rely on. This supplies the missing triggers and requires the start to be recorded. It changes neither of the two triggers the Constitution already states.

## The rule

1. **A deliberation period begins when the proposer marks the proposal open and posts it to the governance channel**, with a link to it and the tier or layer claimed.

2. On GitHub, marking a proposal open means taking the pull request out of draft. A proposal opened without ever being a draft is open from the moment it is created.

3. **A Tier B proposal states its period when it opens.** Section 2 requires a stated period of less than seven days, and a period that was never stated cannot have elapsed.

4. **A period that cannot be shown to have begun has not begun.** However long a proposal has been visible, and however much it has been discussed, its window runs from a recorded opening or not at all.

5. A material change reopens the period, as Section 2 already provides. A correction that does not change meaning does not.

6. **Amendments to the Constitution are unaffected.** Their clock starts on the second endorsing review, as Section 2 provides, and nothing here alters that.

7. Where a member does not use GitHub, the Convener marks the proposal open on their behalf and records it, so that the route Section 1 guarantees costs that member nothing.

## What this does not decide

Whether proposals opened **before this rule** had their periods started — by an automated relay into the governance channel or otherwise — is a question about particular proposals, not a rule. It is for a Record Keeper to judge in each case, and this rule takes no view.

## Ends

**Ends:** 2026-11-30T23:59:00+00:00 — when superseded, or at the end of this Constitution, whichever comes first

`Observed by:` A proposal's opening is recorded on the proposal itself and relayed to the governance channel, so the date a period began, and whether it has elapsed, is answerable from the Record by any member. A proposal with no recorded opening is observably unopened, which is the point of clause 4.
