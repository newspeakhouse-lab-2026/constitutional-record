# Rule: Interim room booking system

This follows the experiment on the booking system. It formalises LibreBooking as our official booking tool and covers only the interim arrangement for using it. The booking policy itself (who gets priority, how much notice is needed, how rooms are shared fairly, what happens at peak times) will be decided separately, as its own proposal.

Tier A (48 hours), because this covers the day-to-day running of Newspeak House rooms.

## What this rule covers

1. **The tool.** Room bookings are made in LibreBooking, an open-source booking system. It currently runs on my home server and will move to the cohort's cloud server (see `rule-cloud-server-hosting.md`) at booking.2026.newspeak.house.
2. **Interim credits.** Each member gets a monthly allowance of booking credits: Lab Members 50, Faculty 35. The balance resets on the 1st of each month and unused credits do not carry over. An hour costs 0.5 credits off-peak and 1 credit at peak (18:00 to midnight every day, and all day at weekends in Newspeak Hall). The Kitchen costs 0.5 credits an hour at all times. The Guest Room costs 1 credit per night. Credits cannot be bought.
3. **First come, first served.** A room goes to whoever books it first. There is no priority between members and no approval step.
4. **House events.** Events on the newspeak.house calendar are copied into the booking system automatically every morning and take precedence, because they are the house's own use of its rooms.

## What this rule does not cover

The credit amounts and prices above are placeholders to get the system working, not a decision on how rooms should be shared. The booking policy will replace this rule, and may change any of these numbers, add approval for some rooms, or drop credits altogether.

## Administration

I am the administrator until a booking policy says otherwise. Admin access is used to fix errors, onboard members and apply this rule, not to allocate rooms at the administrator's discretion.

## Ends

When a booking policy is adopted, or at the end of this Constitution (30 November 2026), whichever comes first.
