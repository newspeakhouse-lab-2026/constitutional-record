# Rule: Ration Club third-party events

**Layer:** 2 — operates within the existing frame; creates no office, spending
or governance structure.
**Status:** live

**Ends:** 23:59 UK time, Tuesday 10 November 2026 (`2026-11-10T23:59:00+00:00`) — provisional;
set the exact instant when this carries. Emergency resolution (§2), expires seven
days after adoption.

## 1.

Large or structured meetings organised by people outside the Laboratory do not
run during Ration Club without the Laboratory's approval. Approval is decided by
majority vote of those who vote, with the same quorum as an Ordinary rule
(Constitution §2, Tier C). Organisers of structured events are directed to the
House's booking arrangements for the downstairs hall.

## 2.

The Agent may communicate this rule in the Laboratory's name and may ask that a
meeting planned during Ration Club be rearranged.

**Observed by:** none — creates no enforceable duty.
