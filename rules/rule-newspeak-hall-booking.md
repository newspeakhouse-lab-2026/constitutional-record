# Rule: Newspeak Hall bookings are requests, confirmed by Ed

**Tier:** A (48 hours)

I'd like us to record how Newspeak Hall is booked through the room booking system. Ed has agreed to this arrangement.

## Why

Newspeak House's Event Space is governed by the college, not by the Laboratory. Ed's guidance is that availability is something to negotiate with him, and that people typically bring him event ideas which he workshops with them. The booking system currently confirms a booking the moment it is made, which is the wrong behaviour for the hall.

## Proposed

1. **Every Newspeak Hall booking is a request.** A booking made in the booking system for Newspeak Hall is not confirmed until Ed confirms it. The system shows it as pending.
2. **A pending request holds the slot.** Nobody else can book the hall for that time while the request is pending, and the schedule shows it as taken.
3. **Ed is told by email** whenever a request is made, and decides in the system by approving or rejecting it. The member is emailed when the request is approved or rejected. A rejected request is removed and the slot is freed.
4. **Every request carries a short description:** what the event is, who it is for, expected numbers, and whether it is public or private.
5. **Ed's own bookings and the public events synced from newspeak.house are confirmed automatically** and do not wait for approval.
6. **Commercial events are not booked through the system.** Enquiries for commercial events go to Ed directly.
7. **If Ed is away,** he may name a delegate to confirm requests while he is away. Until then, requests stay pending.

Everything in this rule applies to Newspeak Hall only. Other spaces, credits and the Guest Room are covered separately.

Observed by: every request, approval and rejection is recorded in the booking system, which any Record Keeper can read, and the hall schedule shows pending and confirmed bookings to every member.

## Alternatives

- **Members agree dates with Ed first, then book.** This keeps the system simple but nothing stops a member booking the hall without asking, and the slot is not held while they talk.
- **Hall bookings stay instant, with Ed able to cancel afterwards.** This is quicker for members but leaves bookings unconfirmed in practice and puts the burden on Ed to find them.

If you see a better arrangement, please say so in the thread.
