# Rule: Disclaimer in Dispatches

1. Every edition of *Dispatches*, the Laboratory's newsletter, carries this text in its footer:

   > Disclaimer: Views expressed in *Dispatches* are those of the individual contributors. They do not necessarily represent the views of Newspeak House, its faculty, or other fellowship candidates.

2. "Dispatches" in the disclaimer is set in italics.

3. The member who publishes an edition ensures the disclaimer is included.

Observed by: the footer of each published edition, visible to any reader in the newsletter archive.
