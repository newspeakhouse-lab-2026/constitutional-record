# Management of the NH book collection

Layer: 2
Tier: A

***Who can borrow books?***
Anyone on the Access Register

***Physical Collection***
Unless borrowed, books the NH collection books shall stay in the House’s Library.
Books acquired by the library should be stamped with the NH logo.
Book donations are welcome when deemed particularly relevant to NH and the Laboratory.

***Digital Library***
The lab deploys and maintains a digital library at this address:
<https://2026.newspeak.house/library/>
Its purpose is to make it easier to navigate, manage, and encourage the use of the NH book collection.

***Public visitors can only view and explore the digital collection.***
Logged-in users can borrow, edit, rate, save, or comment existing books, as well as add new books to the current collection or wish list.   Login is currently administered through a single shared password. Entering your name consistently will help others to know who you are and enable you to keep track of your saved/borrowed books more easily.

***How to borrow?***

- Find the book you’d like to borrow in the physical and digital libraries. The physical library is organised alphabetically, and the digital library supports text search and barcode scanning.
- Mark it as borrowed

***How to return the book?***

- Put it in back in the physical library
- Mark the digital item as returned on the digital library
- Consider commenting on the book’s card with your highlights, takeaways etc. as well as rating it.

***How to suggest a new book?***

- Double-check that the book isn’t already in the collection or suggested
- If it is not, you can add a new book to the wish list by clicking on “+ Add a book”
- You can also upvote other books on the wish list.

***How to add a new book?***

- Scan its barcode or enter its title/isbn
- if the book doesn’t already exist, you can add to the ‘current’ list once logged in
- Ideally, add a stamp to it and place it in the right place on the bookshelf - or let it on the nearby table for a librarian to  take care of it.

***“# Library” Channel***
In addition to the digital library, a conversation channel on the Laboratory’s may be used for additional communication about the NH collection or library system.
